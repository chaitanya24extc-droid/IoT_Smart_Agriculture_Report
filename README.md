## Abstract
Global agriculture faces mounting pressure to increase food yield while contending with freshwater scarcity, soil degradation, and climate volatility. This research report examines how the Internet of Things (IoT) transforms conventional farming into data-driven Precision Agriculture through a four-layer architecture (Perception, LPWAN Network, Edge/Cloud Processing, and Actuation). Key real-world deployments—including closed-loop soil-moisture irrigation, automated greenhouse microclimate control, variable-rate NPK fertilization, and biometric livestock tracking—are analyzed alongside wireless protocols (LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, MQTT) and future Edge-AI trends.
